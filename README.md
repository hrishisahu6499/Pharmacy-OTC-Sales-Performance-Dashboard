# Pharmacy-OTC-Sales-Performance-Dashboard
An interactive Excel Sales Dashboard analyzing OTC pharmacy data ($58K+ revenue) to track KPIs, monthly/weekly trends, and regional performance.  
• Tools: Pivot Tables, Charts, Cross-filtering Slicers. 
• Impact: Connects pharmaceutical domain expertise with actionable data insights to optimize retail inventory and sales performance.
## 💡 Key Business Insights Discovered
* **Top Product Driver:** Digestive Enzymes lead both order volume and total revenue, signaling a priority for stock management and inventory buffering.
* **Geographic Concentration:** The USA accounts for the largest share of global OTC orders, highlighting a key market for targeted marketing campaigns.
* **Seasonality:** Sales display clear fluctuations across months, allowing the pharmacy supply chain to optimize stock levels ahead of peak seasons (May and July) to avoid stockouts.
